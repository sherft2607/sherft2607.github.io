About

Computation, BIM and the buildings in between.

I’m a computational designer in the CORE Modeling Group at Thornton Tomasetti’s CORE studio, where I apply advanced computational and BIM methods to high-complexity projects — from supertall skyscrapers and international airports to major league sports venues and cultural institutions.

I hold a Master of Science in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon, and a Bachelor’s in Architecture and a Master’s in Construction Management from Illinois Tech. Along the way I’ve worked with KPF, Gensler, PALMA and SOBHA, and my research sits where robotics and virtual reality meet architecture.
